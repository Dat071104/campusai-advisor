## Summary

This document captures official facts for the **Khoa học máy tính** program at Ton Duc Thang University.

It also preserves advanced-program details that are explicitly mentioned on the source page.

## Key Facts

- Program: **Khoa học máy tính**.
- Major code: **7480101**.
- Standard program duration: **4 years**.
- The curriculum includes **Information Systems** and **Intelligent Computing** if supported by the source text.
- Intelligent Computing focuses on **AI**, **machine learning**, and **data science** for **Industry 4.0** applications.
- Advanced Program requirement: international **TOEIC 600 or higher** for graduation.
- Advanced Program format: about **30%** of advanced courses are taught in English.
- Advanced Program class size: **20-50 students**.

## Details

The Computer Science page should be used for official program facts and advanced-program details only.
