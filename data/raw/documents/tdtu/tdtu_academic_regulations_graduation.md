## Key Facts

- Undergraduate training is organized under a **credit system**.
- A standard academic year has **two main semesters**.
- Each main semester has **15 study weeks** and **2-4 exam weeks**.
- Supplementary semesters may include **summer** and **auditing** semesters.
- Summer semester maximum: **18 credits**.
- Auditing semester maximum: **9 credits**.
- In main semesters, students may register up to **1.5 times** the average credit load of a standard semester.
- Graduation requires passing all **mandatory courses**, accumulating required credits for **elective groups**, and satisfying the **program learning outcomes**.
- Registration for **Capstone Projects**, **Graduation Theses**, or **Occupational Internships** requires meeting prerequisite conditions.
