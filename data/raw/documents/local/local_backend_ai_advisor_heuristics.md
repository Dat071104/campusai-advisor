## Summary

This is a practical study roadmap for Vietnamese CS students interested in Backend + AI Integration.

It is heuristic guidance, not official TDTU policy.

## Key Facts

- Before Machine Learning: **Python**, **Linear Algebra**, **Probability/Statistics**, **Data Structures and Algorithms**, and **data handling**.
- Before Backend Engineering: **programming fundamentals**, **Git**, **Linux**, **HTTP**, **REST APIs**, **databases**, **authentication**, one **backend framework**, and **Docker basics**.
- Before AI Engineering: **Python**, **ML basics**, **model evaluation**, **APIs**, **vector search/RAG concepts**, and **deployment basics**.
- Before Cybersecurity: **OS**, **networks**, **scripting**, and **web security basics**.
- Year 1: **programming**, **math**, **Git**, **English**, and small projects.
- Year 2: **DSA**, **databases**, **OS**, **networks**, and one serious project.
- Year 3: **internships**, **Docker/cloud basics**, **system design basics**, and a portfolio.
- Year 4: **capstone-quality project**, **interview prep**, and specialization.
- Good projects: **campus advising chatbot**, **internship tracker**, **e-commerce backend**, **document QA chatbot**, and **analytics dashboard**.

## Details

Students aiming for backend or AI work usually benefit from building foundations first, then shipping a few small but complete projects.

A good sequence is to learn core programming, then backend/web systems, then vector search and AI integration.
